## Quick Review
- CTU counts rising edges upward.
- CTD counts rising edges downward after load.
- CTUD counts both directions.
- CV is current value; PV is preset value.
- Use R_TRIG before custom counting logic.
- Use high-speed counters for pulses faster than scan time.
