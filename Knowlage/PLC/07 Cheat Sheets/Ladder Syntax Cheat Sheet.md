## Quick Review
- NO contact: examine TRUE.
- NC contact: examine FALSE.
- Coil writes a boolean.
- Set/reset coils latch and unlatch bits.
- Contacts in series mean AND; parallel branches mean OR.
- Avoid multiple ordinary coils writing the same output.
